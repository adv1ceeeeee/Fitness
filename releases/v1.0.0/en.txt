SportWAI 1.0 — First Release

We're launching SportWAI — a smart workout journal that grows with you.

WHAT THE APP CAN DO

Workouts
• Build programs for any day of the week
• Log sets with weight, reps and RPE during your session
• Rest timer counts automatically
• The app remembers your previous weights and tells you when it's time to progress
• Personal records are captured automatically

Progress
• Workout streak and weekly statistics
• Body weight chart and per-exercise progress graphs
• Body metrics tracking: weight, body fat %, circumferences

Planning
• Calendar with recurring and one-time sessions
• Notifications for workouts, rest days and weigh-ins

Wellness
• Daily check-in: sleep, stress, energy, muscle soreness
• Calorie estimates based on exercise type

Extra
• Deload mode (60% weight with one tap)
• Export workouts to PDF and CSV
• Dark theme, kg/lbs and cm/inches toggle
• PIN lock
