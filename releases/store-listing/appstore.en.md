## Description (4 000 chars)

```
Sportify is a smart strength training log that helps you train with intention.

WHAT'S INSIDE

🏋️ Workouts
• Create programs for any day of the week
• Ready-made templates: Full Body, PPL, Upper/Lower, 5×5 Strength
• Log sets with weight, reps, rest, and RPE while you train
• Auto rest timer between sets
• Personal records detected automatically

📊 Analytics
• Streak of consecutive days + weekly stats
• Progress charts per exercise
• Weekly volume by muscle group
• Readiness score based on sleep and energy

🎯 Personalisation
• Program adapts to your goal: mass, strength, fat loss, endurance
• Adaptive load recommendations week to week
• Wellness tracking: sleep, stress, muscle soreness

📅 Planning
• Calendar with recurring and one-off sessions
• Workout reminders at your chosen time
• Rest days built into the program

💪 Body & health
• Measurement log: weight, body fat %, circumferences
• Trend graphs
• Goal-weight forecasting

📤 Your data, your control
• Export your full history to JSON
• One-tap account deletion
• No ads. No selling data.

WHY SPORTIFY

Unlike most fitness apps, we don't push paywalls at you on every screen. Sportify works without a subscription — the free tier covers the essentials.

CONTACT

Terms of Service: https://sportify.app/legal/terms-en/
```
